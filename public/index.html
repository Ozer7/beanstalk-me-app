Beanstalk Me

Turn one photo into a Jack and the Beanstalk picture book starring your child. This version makes the first three pages and the About Me page.

Test mode: placeholder pictures, no OpenAI calls.
    1

    Who's the star?

    One clear photo and their name. The photo is only used to draw Jack.

    • Face clearly visible, looking at the camera
    • Good light, no sunglasses or hats
    • Just one person in the photo
    2

    Meet Jack

    Jack is drawn once from the photo, then every page copies him. Make sure he looks right before going on.

    Jack appears here.

    Takes about 40 seconds.

    Does Jack look like them?

    Or fix one thing:

    Jack is set.

    Every page copies this Jack. Use the arrows to see him from other angles and with other feelings.

    3

    The pages

    All three pages are drawn in parallel on the server, each with its own progress bar, then each one is checked automatically. The words are set by the book, never drawn by the AI.

    4

    About me

    The last page of the book. Only the name is needed; the rest is optional.

    5

    Read it, then download

    One PDF, 6 × 9 inches: the three pages with their words, then About Me.

    MCON 368. The photo is sent to OpenAI only to draw the pictures. Your books, and every picture in them, are saved to your Google account on this site, so you can close the tab and come back on any device. Delete a book from My books.
