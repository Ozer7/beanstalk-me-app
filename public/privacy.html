Privacy and terms

Beanstalk Me is a student project for MCON 368 (Touro University). It turns a photo into a Jack and the Beanstalk picture book.

What we keep

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture, to know which books are yours.
  • The photo you upload, the pictures drawn from it, and what you type for the About Me page, saved with your book so you can come back to it.
  • Server logs (which pages were requested, how long pictures took, errors), kept to run and fix the site.

Who sees it

Only you. Your pictures load only for your account. The photo and prompts are sent to OpenAI to draw the pictures; nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Deleting

Delete any book from My books; its photo and pictures are deleted with it. To delete your account entirely, email ozer@student.touro.edu.

Terms

Use a photo you have the right to use. The site is provided as-is for a class, with no guarantee it stays online.
